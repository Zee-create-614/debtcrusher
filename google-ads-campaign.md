## 7. Bidding Strategy Recommendation

### Phase 1: Launch (First 2 weeks)
**Strategy:** Maximize Conversions with Target CPA
- **Target CPA:** $4.00
- **Rationale:** Stay under $5 CAC while gathering conversion data
- **Daily Budget Distribution:** 70% Credit Repair, 30% Medical Bills

### Phase 2: Optimization (Weeks 3-8)
**Strategy:** Target CPA Bidding
- **Target CPA:** $3.50 (lower as you optimize)
- **Bid Adjustments:**
  - Mobile: -20% (typically lower conversion rates)
  - Tablet: -10%
  - Desktop: Baseline
  - Time of Day: +20% during 6 PM - 11 PM (decision-making hours)
  - Day of Week: +15% weekends (research time)

### Phase 3: Scale (Week 9+)
**Strategy:** Maximize Conversion Value
- **Target ROAS:** 200% minimum (spend $1, make $2)
- **Rationale:** $9.99 product with healthy margin allows aggressive scaling

### Geographic Targeting:
- **Start:** All US states
- **Exclude:** Alaska, Hawaii (lower search volumes, higher CPCs)
- **Performance-Based:** Add state-level bid adjustments after 2 weeks of data

## 8. Expected Metrics & Performance Projections

### Month 1 Projections (Conservative)
**Budget:** $30/day × 30 days = $900

**Expected Performance:**
- **Impressions:** 25,000 - 35,000
- **Clicks:** 500 - 750 (CTR: 2.0-2.5%)
- **Average CPC:** $1.20 - $1.80
- **Conversions:** 150 - 200
- **Conversion Rate:** 25-30% (high-intent keywords + $9.99 price point)
- **Cost Per Conversion:** $4.50 - $6.00 initially
- **Revenue:** $1,485 - $1,980
- **ROAS:** 165% - 220%

### Month 2-3 Projections (Optimized)
**Expected Performance:**
- **Average CPC:** $1.00 - $1.50 (improved Quality Scores)
- **CTR:** 2.5% - 3.5% (ad optimization)
- **Conversion Rate:** 30% - 40% (landing page optimization)
- **Cost Per Conversion:** $3.00 - $4.50
- **ROAS:** 220% - 330%

### Key Performance Indicators to Track:
1. **Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)** - Target: Under $5.00
2. **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)** - Target: Above 200%
3. **Quality Score** - Target: 7+ across all keywords
4. **Click-Through Rate (CTR)** - Target: Above 2.5%
5. **Conversion Rate** - Target: Above 25%
6. **Search Impression Share** - Target: Above 50% for top keywords

### Weekly Optimization Schedule:
- **Monday:** Review weekend performance, adjust bids
- **Wednesday:** Keyword performance analysis, add negatives
- **Friday:** Ad copy performance review, pause underperformers
- **Weekly:** Search term report analysis, add new keywords

### Red Flags to Watch:
- **CPA above $6.00:** Pause and optimize immediately
- **CTR below 1.5%:** Rewrite ad copy
- **Quality Score below 5:** Improve ad relevance and landing page
- **Conversion Rate below 20%:** Landing page needs work
- **High bounce rate:** Landing page mismatch with ads
